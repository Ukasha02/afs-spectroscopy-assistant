- Surface state and globule size shift the signal; temperature and texture effects need testing [S01 p.17].
- No method or comparison is guaranteed to perform better. *(assumption)*
- Accuracy, update rate, interface ratings and equipment compatibility are unconfirmed. *(assumption)*

### First feasibility experiment

1. Collect representative samples across the agreed fat and protein range and temperature and texture variation.
2. Record spectra from each candidate with independent fat and protein references and timing.
3. Build single-method models first; validate on held-out batches before any combined model.
4. Compare prediction error, bias and ability to follow composition changes against client requirements.

**Decision basis.** Agree accuracy, bias and response targets with the client before testing; choose the simplest configuration meeting them on independent batches.
