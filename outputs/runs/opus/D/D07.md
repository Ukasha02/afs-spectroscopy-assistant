### Risks and uncertainty

- A change of API, polymer or formulation can invalidate a calibration [DOCX §5].
- Temperature, pressure or scattering changes can mimic composition. *(general knowledge)*
- Build-up on the probe may cause drift; verify experimentally. *(general knowledge)*
- Neither method nor a combination is guaranteed to help [DOCX §3].
- Accuracy, update rate, probe ratings and compatibility are unconfirmed. *(assumption)*

### First feasibility experiment

1. Prepare melts across the client-agreed API range and temperature, throughput and formulation variation.
2. Record NIR and MIR-ATR spectra with time-aligned offline assay references.
3. Build single-method models first, combine only if justified; validate on held-out runs [S01 p.8].
4. Compare prediction error, bias and ability to follow changes against client requirements.

**Decision basis.** Agree numerical acceptance criteria with the client before testing; choose the simplest configuration meeting them on independent runs.
