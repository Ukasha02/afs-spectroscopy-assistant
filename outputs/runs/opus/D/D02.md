### Recommendation

- **NIR spectroscopy (diffuse reflection probe).** The dairy example supports inline NIR reflection for opaque, concentrated products [S01 p.17]. Light penetrates shallowly, so the signal is sensitive to surface and fat globule size [S01 p.17].
- **NIR spectroscopy (transflection probe).** In some dairy cases, transflection is described as a combined solution for low- and high-fat milk products; to be tested [S01 p.18]. Transmission-type paths can fail when the product is too thick or fat-rich [S01 p.18].

**Measurement concept.** Compare NIR diffuse reflection and transflection probes on representative samples [S01 Table 1 (p.6)]. Flush mounting in pipe or vat, per the dairy example; point depends on access [S01 p.17]. No link stated; Vis-NIR silica transmits 350–2400 nm, below the 2500 nm NIR limit [S02 p.6; S01 p.10].
