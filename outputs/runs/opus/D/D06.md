### Recommendation

- **Premise: A fiber-based measurement closer to the process can show water changes sooner than laboratory sampling.** — plausible to be tested. Online analysis avoids sampling delays. The AFS slide shows only ATR spectra of oil with 0.1-2.0% water, not a validated performance result [S01 p.3; S02 p.13].
- **Premise: The analyzer can sit outside the process area, linked by fiber to the measuring point.** — unverifiable. Fiber family and link length are not stated. Silica fibers (350-2400 nm) cannot carry mid-IR, which needs CIR or PIR fibers [S02 p.6]. Long silica cables are shown for remote process control. No link lengths are given for mid-IR fibers [S02 p.7].
- **MIR (FTIR) spectroscopy (ATR probe).** The AFS slide shows ATR spectra of oil with 0.1-2.0% water, with absorbance rising with water content. It gives no validated performance [S02 p.13]. Micron-deep contact sampling makes fouling critical, and a remote link needs mid-IR fibers [S01 Table 1 (p.6); S02 p.6].
- **NIR spectroscopy (transmission or transflection probe).** An AFS band slide lists NIR bands for moisture, and silica cables support remote process control [S02 p.18; S02 p.7]. There is no petroleum example; transmission suits clear liquids, and sensitivity at low water levels must be tested [S01 p.18; S01 p.16].

**Measurement concept.** MIR ATR probe in contact with the liquid; NIR transmission or transflection probe for comparison [S01 Table 1 (p.6)]. Probe near the process; analyzer outside the process area. The link is undefined. Silica (350-2400 nm) cannot carry mid-IR; CIR (1100-6500 nm) or PIR (3000 nm-17 um) is needed [S02 p.6]. No fiber link stated. ### Risks and uncertainty

- Grade or matrix changes can invalidate a calibration. *(general knowledge)*
- The remote mid-IR link is unverified; silica cannot carry it [S02 p.6].
- Deposits on the micron-deep ATR crystal can distort readings [S01 Table 1 (p.6)].
- Neither method is guaranteed to meet the requirements. *(assumption)*
- Accuracy, update rate, ratings and compatibility are unconfirmed. *(assumption)*

### First feasibility experiment

1. Collect samples across the client-agreed water range and observed grade, temperature and appearance variation.
2. Record MIR-ATR and NIR spectra with time-aligned independent laboratory water results.
3. Build single-method models; validate on held-out runs or independent samples.
4. Compare prediction error, bias and ability to follow changes against the client's requirements.

**Decision basis.** Agree numerical acceptance criteria with the client before testing; choose the simplest configuration that meets them on independent material.
