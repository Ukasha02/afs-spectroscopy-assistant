### Recommendation

- **Premise plausible to be tested:** A fiber-based measurement closer to the process can notice water changes sooner than laboratory sampling [S01 p.3; S02 p.13].
- **Premise unverifiable:** The analyzer can sit outside the process area, connected by a fiber link [S02 p.6; S02 p.7].
- **MIR (FTIR) spectroscopy (ATR probe).** An AFS application slide shows ATR probes for water content in petroleum [S02 p.13]. The general fiber chart shows silica does not reach mid-IR, so the remote link must be confirmed for this application [S02 p.6].
- **NIR spectroscopy (transmission or transflection probe).** NIR suits remote fiber measurement with standard glass optics [S01 p.5]. Overlapping bands need a chemometric calibration; no petroleum water example is supplied [S01 p.10].

**Measurement concept.** Compare an MIR ATR probe with an NIR transmission or transflection probe on liquid samples [S01 Table 1 (p.6)]. Probe at a process point; analyzer outside the process area. No fiber link stated. Rule check: MIR: Vis-NIR silica no coverage; polycrystalline IR (PIR) partial (3-17 um); NIR: Vis-NIR silica partial (0.78-2.4 um) [S02 p.6; S01 p.9; S01 p.10]. Needs AFS confirmation: Probe type and tip, Installation and ratings, Fiber family and length.

**Spectral range.** MIR (FTIR): 4000-400 cm-1 nominal [S01 p.9] (Usable range limited by fiber and crystal); NIR: 780-2500 nm nominal [S01 p.10] (Nominal; select bands from sample spectra). Usable range depends on the selected instrument and fiber; no optimal band without sample spectra.

### Risks and uncertainty

- Grade or matrix changes may degrade a calibration. *(general knowledge)*
- Mid-IR fiber reach to the remote analyzer is unconfirmed [S02 p.6].
- ATR depth is 1-6 um; deposits could bias results (inference) [S01 Table 1 (p.6)] *(assumption)*.
- Neither method is guaranteed to meet the client's needs. *(assumption)*
- Accuracy, update rate, ratings and compatibility are unconfirmed. *(assumption)*

### First feasibility experiment

1. Collect samples across the client-agreed water range, grades, temperatures and appearance states.
2. Record MIR-ATR and NIR spectra with time-aligned laboratory water reference results.
3. Build single-method models; validate on held-out batches or independent material.
4. Compare prediction error, bias and the ability to follow changes against the client's requirements and link feasibility.

**Decision basis.** Agree numerical acceptance criteria before the test; choose the simplest configuration that meets them on independent material.
