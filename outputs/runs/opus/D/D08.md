### Recommendation

- **MIR spectroscopy (ATR probe (contact)).** In a laboratory chicken-tissue study, IR was reported to discriminate fat best; no claim is made for the group's samples [S04 p.4]. ATR needs tight contact with soft samples [S04 p.4].
- **Fluorescence spectroscopy (Fluorimetric probe (contact)).** The same laboratory chicken-tissue study reported fluorescence suited to liver recognition; other samples may differ [S04 p.4]. Fluorescence needs a fluorescent component and has low selectivity [S04 p.4; S01 Table 1 (p.6)].
- **MIR-ATR + fluorescence (Combi probe, same spot).** In the chicken-tissue lab study, only the fused model separated all three classes [S04 p.4]. Judge only after each method has been assessed on its own; the gain is a hypothesis to test [S01 p.7; DOCX §5].

**Measurement concept.** MIR-ATR and fluorimetric probes, ideally one combi probe sharing the ATR crystal head for same-spot measurement [S01 Table 1 (p.6); S04 p.2]. Laboratory bench; probe in contact at marked tissue positions. No fiber link stated; match the fiber per channel to the actual probe configuration [S02 p.6]. No fiber link stated. Rule check: MIR: Vis-NIR silica no coverage; polycrystalline IR (PIR) partial (3-17 um); FLUO: UV-Vis silica covers the nominal range [S02 p.6; S01 p.9; S01 p.12]. Needs AFS confirmation: Combi probe and crystal, Fiber type per channel, Fluorescence excitation wavelength.

**Spectral range.** MIR (ATR): 4000-400 cm-1 nominal [S01 p.9] (Usable range set by fiber and crystal); Fluorescence: UV and visible regions; species-dependent nominal [S01 p.12] (Excitation chosen from sample spectra). Usable range depends on the selected instrument and fiber; no optimal band without sample spectra.

### Risks and uncertainty

- A classifier may fail for other tissues or preparations [DOCX §5].
- In the chicken study, fat inhomogeneity lowered reproducibility [S04 p.3].
- Neither method nor fusion is guaranteed to improve classification [DOCX §5].
- Classification performance, signal quality and compatibility are unconfirmed. *(assumption)*
- Results show lab model-tissue classification only, not clinical readiness [DOCX §3].

### First feasibility experiment

1. Prepare independent pieces per tissue class covering marbling, freshness and preparation variation; mark positions.
2. Record same-spot and sequential spectra per position with independently assigned class labels and timestamps [S01 p.8].
3. Build single-method classifiers first, fused only if justified; validate on held-out pieces or batches.
4. Compare prediction error, bias and the ability to follow class changes against the group's criteria.

**Decision basis.** Agree numerical success criteria before testing; choose the simplest configuration meeting them on independent pieces, claiming only lab model-tissue classification.
