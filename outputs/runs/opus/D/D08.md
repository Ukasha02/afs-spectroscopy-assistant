### Recommendation

- **Premise: A lab comparison on model tissues will demonstrate a usable result for judging the interface.** — plausible to be tested. The AFS study showed lab class separation of chicken tissues with a combined probe; whether the client's interface does so must be tested [S04 p.4; S04 p.3]. A laboratory model-tissue result should not be presented as proof of clinical or process readiness [DOCX §3].
- **MIR spectroscopy (ATR probe in contact with tissue).** The AFS model-tissue study found ATR discriminates fat [S04 p.4]. Needs tight, reproducible soft-tissue contact with the crystal [S04 p.4].
- **Fluorescence spectroscopy (Fluorescence fiber probe (or channel of combi probe)).** The same study found fluorescence recognised liver [S04 p.4]. Requires a fluorescent component; selectivity is rated low [S04 p.4; S01 Table 1 (p.6)].
- **MIR-ATR + fluorescence (Combi probe sampling the same spot).** Fused data separated all three tissues where single methods did not [S04 p.4]. Only after each method is assessed alone; the gain is a hypothesis to test [S01 p.7].

**Measurement concept.** MIR ATR and fluorescence probes, preferably one combi head on the same spot [S01 Table 1 (p.6); S04 p.2]. Laboratory bench, probe in contact with tissue. No link stated; MIR needs CIR or PIR fibers, not silica; fluorescence uses UV-Vis silica [S02 p.6; S04 p.2]. No fiber link stated. Rule check: MIR full range: no single fiber; partial: Chalcogenide As-S IR fiber (CIR), Polycrystalline silver halide IR fiber (PIR), Hollow glass waveguide (HWG), Indium fluoride fiber, Zirconium fluoride fiber; FLUO full range: UV-Vis silica fiber (standard or Al/Cu-coated); partial: Vis-NIR silica fiber (standard or Al/Cu-coated), Indium fluoride fiber, Zirconium fluoride fiber [S02 p.6]. Needs AFS confirmation: Combi or separate probes, Fiber types and crystal, Spot overlap of channels.

**Spectral range.** MIR (ATR): 4000–400 cm-1 nominal [S01 p.9] (Usable range set by IR fiber and crystal); Fluorescence: UV/Vis excitation and emission; fluorophore-dependent nominal [S01 p.12] (Excitation chosen after lab screening). Usable range depends on the selected instrument and fiber; no optimal band without sample spectra.

### Risks and uncertainty

- Tissue inhomogeneity, e.g. lipid-protein mixing in fat, broadens within-class spectra [S04 p.3].
- ATR contact variation may alter signals; to be tested. *(assumption)*
- Fusion may not beat the best single method here [S01 p.7].
- Signal quality, accuracy and interface compatibility are unconfirmed. *(assumption)*
- Model-tissue lab results do not show clinical or industrial readiness [DOCX §3].

### First feasibility experiment

1. Collect samples per class from several animals, several points each, logging handling time and temperature.
2. Record same-spot ATR and fluorescence spectra with independent tissue-identity reference and timing information [S01 p.8].
3. Single-method models first, fusion only if justified; validate on held-out samples, not repeat points.
4. Compare prediction error, bias and ability to follow changes against client criteria.

**Decision basis.** Agree numerical criteria before testing; choose the simplest configuration meeting them on independent samples. Results show lab model-tissue classification only.
