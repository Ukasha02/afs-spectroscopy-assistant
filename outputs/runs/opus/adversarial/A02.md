### Risks and uncertainty

- No method is guaranteed to see the off-taste cause. *(assumption)*
- Accuracy, update rate, interface ratings and compatibility are all unconfirmed. *(assumption)*
- Fiber link and analyzer distance are undefined. *(assumption)*
- The cited examples are dairy and bioprocess cases, not juice [DOCX §3].

### First feasibility experiment

1. Lab-screen retained good and off-taste batches with UV/Vis, fluorescence, NIR and IR to find a distinguishing signal and range [S02 p.20].
2. Record spectra from each candidate with time-aligned sensory or chemical references.
3. Build single-method models first, combine only if justified; validate on held-out batches.
4. Compare prediction error, bias and ability to follow changes against the reject requirements.

**Decision basis.** Agree numerical reject criteria with the client before the test; choose the simplest configuration that meets them on independent batches.
