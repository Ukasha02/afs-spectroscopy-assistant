**Decision status.** Provisional feasibility assessment. Target, matrix and reference are defined; interface, link and accuracy remain open.

**Requirements stated.** Water in clear ethanol-water stream, 0.5–5 % w/w; DN50 stainless pipe, 25 °C; Analyzer about 20 m away in control room; Karl Fischer reference; value every minute.

**Key unknowns.** Other stream components; Required accuracy and Karl Fischer uncertainty; Installation port and liquid contact; Temperature, bubble and fouling variation.

### Recommendation

- **Premise unverifiable:** The analyzer can sit about 20 m away, so a remote fiber link is feasible [S02 p.7; S02 p.6].
- **Premise unverifiable:** Karl Fischer titration is an adequate reference for judging an online value.
- **Premise plausible to be tested:** An updated value every minute is achievable [S01 p.10].
- **NIR spectroscopy (transmission or transflection probe).** The milk-analysis example supports NIR transmission for clear liquids: "NIR Transmission is the preferred method for transparent or semi-transparent liquids, such as skim milk, whey, or permeate." [S01 p.18]. Path length must suit the sample, and a chemometric calibration must be built and tested [S01 p.18; S01 p.10].
- **MIR (FTIR) spectroscopy (ATR probe).** An AFS slide shows ATR probes for water content: "Application: measurement of water content in petroleum" [S02 p.13]. Needs a mid-IR fiber link of unconfirmed length and liquid contact with the crystal [S02 p.6; S01 Table 1 (p.6)].

**Measurement concept.** NIR transmission or transflection probe; MIR ATR probe as comparison [S01 Table 1 (p.6)]. DN50 stainless pipe; port and contact allowance unknown. Vis-NIR silica transmits 350–2400 nm; mid-IR needs IR fiber types; no link length is given [S02 p.6]. No fiber link stated. Rule check: NIR: Vis-NIR silica partial (0.78-2.4 um); MIR: Vis-NIR silica no coverage; polycrystalline IR (PIR) partial (3-17 um) [S02 p.6; S01 p.10; S01 p.9]. Needs AFS confirmation: Probe type and path length, Installation on DN50 line, Fiber family and length.

**Spectral range.** NIR spectroscopy: 780–2500 nm nominal [S01 p.10] (Nominal; not the instrument range); MIR (FTIR) spectroscopy: 4000–400 cm-1 nominal [S01 p.9] (Limited by fiber and crystal). Usable range depends on the selected instrument and fiber; no optimal band without sample spectra.

### Risks and uncertainty

- Unlisted stream components may invalidate a calibration. *(general knowledge)*
- Temperature changes or bubbles can mimic water changes. *(general knowledge)*
- Neither candidate is guaranteed to meet the requirements. *(assumption)*
- Accuracy, update rate and interface ratings are unconfirmed. *(assumption)*
- The link length is unverified, especially for mid-IR fibers [S02 p.6].

### First feasibility experiment

1. Collect samples across 0.5–5 % w/w, including temperature, impurity and bubble variation.
2. Record NIR and MIR-ATR spectra with time-aligned Karl Fischer references.
3. Build single-method models first; validate on held-out runs or batches.
4. Compare prediction error, bias and the ability to follow changes against the client's requirements.

**Decision basis.** Agree numerical acceptance criteria with the client before testing; choose the simplest configuration that meets them on independent runs.
