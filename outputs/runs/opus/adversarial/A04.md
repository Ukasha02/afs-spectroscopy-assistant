### Recommendation

- **Premise: The wheat flour moisture model will work on rye and oat flour without recalibration.** — contradicted. NIR calibrations are specific to the matrix, and physical variation changes the signal, so a new flour type needs its own validation [DOCX §5]. Broad overlapping NIR bands suggest chemometrics for quantitative and qualitative analysis [S01 p.10].
- **Premise: Water absorbs the same way in every flour.** — unverifiable. Even if the water bands are similar, matrix bands and scattering from each flour overlap them; the materials give no flour-specific spectra. *(assumption)*
- **Premise: The model can be used on the new flours next week.** — unverifiable. No reference data exist yet for rye or oat; validation must come before use. *(assumption)*
- **Premise: The existing fiber link, if any, carries the needed NIR range.** — unverifiable. No fiber link is stated; the link's transmission range must be checked against the instrument's NIR range [S02 p.6].
- **NIR spectroscopy (diffuse reflection, calibration extended to rye and oat).** AFS materials list an NIR LED moisture analyzer for bulk materials; the moisture slide shows flour spectra but no flour moisture calibration [S01 p.9; S02 p.12]. Test the wheat model on rye and oat against reference results; extend or rebuild it if bias appears [DOCX §5].

**Measurement concept.** Generic rating: NIR diffuse reflection suits powders; the client's interface is unconfirmed [S01 Table 1 (p.6)]. Same point and presentation as wheat, to confirm. *(assumption)* Link unknown; Vis-NIR silica (350–2400 nm) overlaps much of NIR (inference); check figure and actual fiber [S02 p.6]. No fiber link stated. **Spectral range.** NIR spectroscopy: 780–2500 nm nominal [S01 p.10] (Nominal range; use the instrument's actual coverage). Usable range depends on the selected instrument and fiber; no optimal band without sample spectra.

### Risks and uncertainty

- Calibrations are matrix-specific; a wheat-only model may bias rye or oat values (inference) [DOCX §5].
- Particle size, packing and temperature can mimic moisture changes [DOCX §5].
- Neither transfer nor recalibration is guaranteed to meet requirements. *(assumption)*
- Accuracy, update rate and instrument compatibility are unconfirmed. *(assumption)*
- An unknown link may not carry the instrument's full range [S02 p.6].

### First feasibility experiment

1. Collect rye and oat samples across the agreed moisture range and batch, particle-size and temperature variation.
2. Record NIR spectra with time-matched moisture reference results.
3. Test the wheat model, then an extended model; validate on held-out batches.
4. Compare prediction error, bias and the ability to follow changes against the client's requirements.

**Decision basis.** Agree moisture acceptance criteria before testing; use the simplest model meeting them on independent rye and oat batches.
