### Recommendation

- **Premise: A fiber-based measurement is suitable for water determination in the petroleum stream.** — plausible to be tested. An AFS slide shows ATR probe spectra of oil with 0.1-2.0 % water; an example, not validated performance for this stream [S02 p.13].
- **Premise: The analyzer can sit outside the process area (remote link).** — unverifiable. Fiber family and link length are not stated, so the link cannot be checked. Silica fibers cannot carry mid-IR; mid-IR needs CIR, PIR or HWG fibers [S02 p.6].
- **MIR ATR (ATR probe, with a mid-IR fiber).** An AFS slide shows an ATR probe example for oil with water, a reasonable starting point to test, not a validated choice for this stream [S02 p.13]. ATR samples only about 1-6 um deep; contact and fouling are a general practical concern [S01 Table 1 (p.6)].
- **NIR (Transmission or transflection probe over silica fiber).** NIR is described as suited to online and remote fiber measurement; no petroleum NIR example is in the materials [S01 p.5]. NIR may lack sensitivity for trace compounds below about 0.1 percent, so the water level decides [S01 p.16].

**Measurement concept.** ATR probe for MIR; transmission or transflection probe for NIR; final geometry follows the stream's optics [S01 Table 1 (p.6)]. Closer to the process than lab sampling; exact point unknown. Link unknown. Silica fibers transmit 350-2400 nm; CIR 1.1-6.5 um; PIR 3-17 um. Which suits MIR coverage is an inference; no link lengths given [S02 p.6]. No fiber link stated. Rule check: MIR full range: no single fiber; partial: Chalcogenide As-S IR fiber (CIR), Polycrystalline silver halide IR fiber (PIR), Hollow glass waveguide (HWG), Indium fluoride fiber, Zirconium fluoride fiber; NIR full range: Indium fluoride fiber, Zirconium fluoride fiber; partial: UV-Vis silica fiber (standard or Al/Cu-coated), Vis-NIR silica fiber (standard or Al/Cu-coated), Chalcogenide As-S IR fiber (CIR), Hollow glass waveguide (HWG) [S02 p.6]. Needs AFS confirmation: Mid-IR fiber link length, Probe type and installation, Optical configuration.

**Spectral range.** MIR: 4000-400 cm-1 (2.5-25 um) nominal [S01 p.9] (Usable range depends on fiber); NIR: 780-2500 nm nominal [S01 p.10] (Check instrument and fiber coverage). Usable range depends on the selected instrument and fiber; no optimal band without sample spectra.

### Risks and uncertainty

- A change of petroleum composition can invalidate a calibration. *(general knowledge)*
- Temperature, emulsification or scattering can mimic water changes. *(general knowledge)*
- Neither method is guaranteed to detect the needed water level. *(assumption)*
- Accuracy, response time and compatibility are unconfirmed. *(assumption)*
- Remote mid-IR fiber link is unverified [S02 p.6].

### First feasibility experiment

1. Collect representative samples across the agreed water range and process variation.
2. Record MIR-ATR and NIR spectra with lab water reference and timing; set range and ppm versus % level.
3. Build single-method models first; validate on held-out runs, then trial in-line.
4. Compare prediction error, bias and ability to follow changes against client requirements.

**Decision basis.** Agree numerical acceptance criteria with the client before the test; choose the simplest configuration that meets them on independent material.
