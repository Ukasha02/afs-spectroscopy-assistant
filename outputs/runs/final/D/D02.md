### Recommendation

- **Premise plausible to be tested:** Spectroscopy can give earlier information on fat and protein changes than current sampling [S01 p.17].
- **NIR spectroscopy (diffuse reflection probe, flush-mounted).** The AFS dairy example supports NIR reflection for opaque, concentrated milk products and fat and protein quantification [S01 p.17]. The signal is sensitive to surface characteristics and fat globule size; the calibration must be built and tested [S01 p.17].
- **MIR spectroscopy (ATR probe).** The AFS milk material names MIR ATR as a supplement to NIR for specific chemical information [S01 p.18]. ATR samples only 1-6 um and needs mid-IR fibers; contact and fiber link need checks [S01 Table 1 (p.6); S02 p.6].

**Measurement concept.** NIR diffuse reflection probe as primary, IR ATR probe as comparison; test both on lab samples first [S01 Table 1 (p.6)]. Flush-mounted in pipe or vat at a client-agreed point [S01 p.17]. No link stated. Vis-NIR silica (350-2400 nm) covers NIR up to 2.4 um; silica cannot carry MIR; PIR covers 3-17 um [S02 p.6; S01 p.10; S01 p.9]. No fiber link stated. Rule check: NIR: Vis-NIR silica partial (0.78-2.4 um); MIR: Vis-NIR silica no coverage; polycrystalline IR (PIR) partial (3-17 um) [S02 p.6; S01 p.10; S01 p.9]. Needs AFS confirmation: Probe type and window, Installation and hygienic mounting, Optical configuration and fiber link.

**Spectral range.** NIR spectroscopy: 780–2500 nm nominal [S01 p.10] (Nominal; usable band depends on setup); MIR spectroscopy (ATR): 4000–400 cm-1 nominal [S01 p.9] (Limited by fiber and crystal). Usable range depends on the selected instrument and fiber; no optimal band without sample spectra.

### Risks and uncertainty

- Surface characteristics and fat globule size affect the NIR reflection signal [S01 p.17].
- New products or formulations may invalidate calibrations; to be tested. *(assumption)*
- Crystal deposits may disturb MIR-ATR signals; to be verified. *(assumption)*
- No method or combination is guaranteed to meet requirements. *(general knowledge)*
- Accuracy, update rate, interface ratings and equipment compatibility are unconfirmed. *(general knowledge)*

### First feasibility experiment

1. Collect samples across client-agreed fat and protein ranges, temperature, texture and bubble variation.
2. Record NIR and MIR-ATR spectra with time-aligned independent fat and protein results.
3. Build single-method models first; validate on held-out runs; combine only if justified.
4. Compare prediction error, bias and ability to follow changes against client requirements.

**Decision basis.** Agree numerical acceptance criteria before testing; choose the simplest configuration meeting them on independent runs.
