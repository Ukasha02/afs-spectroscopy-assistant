### Recommendation

- **Premise unverifiable:** No fiber link stated for the MIR-ATR and fluorescence probes [DOCX §5; S04 p.2].
- **MIR spectroscopy (ATR probe in contact with tissue).** In an AFS lab study on chicken tissue, FTIR spectra from the ATR probe separated fat via the ester carbonyl band [S04 p.3; S04 p.4]. ATR needs tight contact with liquid or soft samples and has an optical depth of about 1-6 um [S04 p.4; S01 Table 1 (p.6)].
- **Fluorescence spectroscopy (Fluorimetric probe in contact with tissue).** The same lab study found 'fluorescence is well suited for the recognition of liver tissue' [S04 p.4]. Requires fluorescent components and has low selectivity; the excitation choice for these samples is a point to test [S04 p.4; S01 Table 1 (p.6)].
- **MIR-ATR + fluorescence (Combi probe measuring the same spot).** In one lab study with three samples per chicken tissue type, fused IR and fluorescence data separated all three classes in PCA [S04 p.4; S04 p.3]. Only after each method is assessed alone; a hypothesis to test [DOCX §5].

**Measurement concept.** MIR-ATR and fluorimetric probes, ideally one combi probe reading the same spot [S01 Table 1 (p.6); S04 p.2]. Lab bench, probe tip in contact at several distributed points per sample. No fiber link stated. Rule check: MIR: Vis-NIR silica no coverage; polycrystalline IR (PIR) partial (3-17 um); FLUO: UV-Vis silica covers the nominal range [S02 p.6; S01 p.9; S01 p.12]. Needs AFS confirmation: Combi probe availability, ATR crystal and fibers, Fluorescence excitation wavelength.

**Spectral range.** MIR (ATR): 4000–400 cm-1 (2.5–25 µm) nominal [S01 p.9]; Fluorescence: UV/Vis excitation; species-dependent emission nominal [S01 p.12]. Usable range depends on the selected instrument and fiber; no optimal band without sample spectra.

### Risks and uncertainty

- A model from one tissue set may fail on new material [DOCX §5].
- Fat inhomogeneity lowered IR reproducibility in the lab study [S04 p.3].
- Fusion may not beat the best single method [DOCX §5].
- Accuracy, signal quality and probe compatibility are unconfirmed. *(assumption)*
- Fiber link undefined; each method needs a transmitting fiber [DOCX §5].

### First feasibility experiment

1. Prepare independent samples per agreed class, spanning composition, freshness and heterogeneity.
2. Record same-spot MIR-ATR and fluorescence spectra with reference labels, positions and timestamps.
3. Single-method models first, fused only if justified; validate on held-out animals.
4. Compare prediction error, bias and ability to follow changes against agreed criteria.

**Decision basis.** Agree classification criteria with the client beforehand; choose the simplest configuration meeting them on independent samples.
