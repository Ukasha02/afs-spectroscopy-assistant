### Recommendation

- **MIR spectroscopy (ATR fiber probe).** In a lab chicken-tissue study, ATR IR discriminated fat via the ester carbonyl band: "IR spectroscopy works better for the discrimination of fat" [S04 p.3; S04 p.4]. Needs tight contact with soft tissue and mid-IR, not silica, fibers [S04 p.4; S04 p.2; S02 p.6].
- **Fluorescence spectroscopy (Fluorimetric fiber probe).** In the same lab chicken-tissue study, "fluorescence is well suited for the recognition of liver tissue" [S04 p.4]. Requires a fluorescent component; selectivity is rated low [S04 p.4; S01 Table 1 (p.6)].
- **MIR ATR + fluorescence (Combi probe, same measurement spot).** In that lab study (three samples per tissue type), fused data gave three groups matching tissue types [S04 p.3; S04 p.4]. Only after each method is assessed alone; the gain is a hypothesis to test [S04 p.4; S01 p.7].

**Measurement concept.** ATR probe and fluorimetric probe; AFS proposes a combi ATR/fluorescence probe so both sample the same spot [S01 Table 1 (p.6); S04 p.2; S01 p.8]. Lab bench, probe tip in contact with cut tissue at distributed points. No link stated. MIR needs mid-IR fiber such as PIR; fluorescence uses UV-Vis silica [S02 p.6]. No fiber link stated. Rule check: MIR: Vis-NIR silica no coverage; polycrystalline IR (PIR) partial (3-17 um); FLUO: UV-Vis silica covers the nominal range [S02 p.6; S01 p.9; S01 p.12]. Needs AFS confirmation: Combi probe availability, Fluorescence excitation configuration, Spectrometer and fiber coupling.

**Spectral range.** MIR (ATR): 4000-400 cm-1 nominal [S01 p.9]; Fluorescence: UV/Vis excitation and emission; depends on the fluorescent species nominal [S01 p.12]. Usable range depends on the selected instrument and fiber; no optimal band without sample spectra.

### Risks and uncertainty

- A classifier may not transfer to other tissues or preparations. *(general knowledge)*
- In the reference lab study, fat inhomogeneity lowered reproducibility [S04 p.3].
- Fusion beat single methods only in a small lab study; here unverified [S04 p.4].
- Accuracy, signal quality and equipment compatibility are unconfirmed. *(assumption)*
- Evidence is a lab model-tissue study; clinical or industrial use untested [S04 p.4].

### First feasibility experiment

1. Prepare independent pieces per class across agreed freshness, temperature and surface variation.
2. Record same-spot spectra with independent class reference and timing per sample and point.
3. Single-method classifiers first, fusion only if justified; validate on held-out whole samples.
4. Compare prediction error, bias and ability to follow class changes against agreed criteria.

**Decision basis.** Agree numerical criteria before testing; choose the simplest configuration meeting them on independent samples.
