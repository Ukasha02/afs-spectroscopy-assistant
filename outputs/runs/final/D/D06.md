### Recommendation

- **Premise plausible to be tested:** A fiber-based measurement near the process can reveal water changes sooner than laboratory sampling [S02 p.13; S01 p.3].
- **Premise unverifiable:** The analyzer can sit outside the process area, connected by a fiber link [S02 p.6].
- **MIR (FTIR) spectroscopy (ATR immersion probe).** An AFS slide shows ATR probes for water in petroleum, supporting a test on this stream [S02 p.13]. Silica fibers do not cover mid-IR; a mid-IR-capable fiber (e.g. polycrystalline or chalcogenide) must be confirmed for the remote link [S02 p.6].
- **NIR spectroscopy (Transmission or transflection probe (if the stream is clear)).** NIR runs over silica fibers; robust long silica cables support a remote analyzer [S02 p.7]. No petroleum-water NIR example; NIR may lack trace sensitivity [S01 p.16].

**Measurement concept.** MIR ATR probe in liquid contact; NIR transmission/transflection probe as comparison if the stream is clear [S01 Table 1 (p.6)]. Probe at a representative process point; analyzer outside the process area, as the client stated. No link stated; MIR needs an IR fiber such as chalcogenide or polycrystalline, not silica [S02 p.6]. Needs AFS confirmation: MIR fiber link length, Probe materials and rating, Installation point and access.

**Spectral range.** MIR (ATR): 4000-400 cm-1 nominal [S01 p.9]; NIR: 780-2500 nm nominal [S01 p.10]. Usable range depends on the selected instrument and fiber; no optimal band without sample spectra.

### Risks and uncertainty

- A mid-IR fiber link may not reach the remote analyzer [S02 p.6].
- Petroleum composition changes could invalidate a water calibration. *(general knowledge)*
- ATR probes only micrometres deep, so crystal deposits could distort readings. *(general knowledge)*
- Neither method is guaranteed to meet requirements. *(assumption)*
- Accuracy, update rate, ratings and compatibility are unconfirmed. *(assumption)*

### First feasibility experiment

1. Collect stream samples across the client-agreed water range, composition, temperature and appearance variation.
2. Record MIR-ATR and NIR spectra with time-aligned laboratory water reference results.
3. Build single-method models; validate on held-out runs or independent samples.
4. Compare prediction error, bias and the ability to follow changes against client requirements, including link feasibility.

**Decision basis.** Agree numerical acceptance criteria with the client before testing; choose the simplest configuration meeting them on independent material.
