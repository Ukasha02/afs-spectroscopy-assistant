### Recommendation

- **Premise plausible to be tested:** A fiber-based measurement closer to the process lets water changes be noticed sooner than laboratory sampling [S01 p.3; S02 p.13].
- **Premise unverifiable:** The analyzer can sit outside the process area with a fiber link to the probe [S02 p.6; S02 p.7].
- **MIR (FTIR) spectroscopy (ATR probe).** An AFS slide shows ATR probes for this application: 'Application: measurement of water content in petroleum' [S02 p.13]. ATR samples only micrometres in contact and needs a mid-IR fiber, not silica [S01 Table 1 (p.6); S02 p.6].
- **NIR spectroscopy (Transmission/transflection probe (diffuse reflection if turbid)).** NIR suits remote fiber probes: 'Near-IR vibrational spectroscopy (NIR) is the most common online analysis method performed using fiber optic probes.' [S01 p.10]. The cited materials contain no water-in-petroleum NIR example; overlapping bands suggest chemometric calibration [S01 p.10].

**Measurement concept.** Compare an MIR ATR probe with an NIR transmission/transflection probe; diffuse reflection if turbid [S01 Table 1 (p.6)]. Probe near the process, analyzer outside the process area; installation point to be defined. No fiber link stated; rule check: silica cannot carry MIR, PIR partial; silica partial for NIR [S02 p.6]. No fiber link stated. Rule check: MIR: Vis-NIR silica no coverage; polycrystalline IR (PIR) partial (3-17 um); NIR: Vis-NIR silica partial (0.78-2.4 um) [S02 p.6; S01 p.9; S01 p.10]. Needs AFS confirmation: ATR tip and shaft materials, Mid-IR fiber link length, Probe installation and ratings.

**Spectral range.** MIR (FTIR): 4000-400 cm-1 (2.5-25 um) nominal [S01 p.9]; NIR: 780-2500 nm nominal [S01 p.10]. Usable range depends on the selected instrument and fiber; no optimal band without sample spectra.

### Risks and uncertainty

- Composition changes can invalidate a matrix-specific calibration [DOCX §5].
- Mid-IR link to a distant analyzer is unconfirmed; silica cannot carry mid-IR [S02 p.6].
- Deposits or droplets on the ATR window may bias readings. *(assumption)*
- Neither candidate is guaranteed to meet the client's needs. *(assumption)*
- Accuracy, update rate, interface ratings and equipment compatibility are unconfirmed. *(assumption)*

### First feasibility experiment

1. Collect stream samples across the client-agreed water range, phases, composition and temperature variation.
2. Record MIR-ATR and NIR spectra with time-aligned laboratory water reference values.
3. Build single-method models first and validate them on held-out batches or independent samples.
4. Compare prediction error, bias and ability to follow water changes against the client's requirements.

**Decision basis.** Agree numerical acceptance criteria with the client before the test; choose the simplest configuration that meets them on independent material.
