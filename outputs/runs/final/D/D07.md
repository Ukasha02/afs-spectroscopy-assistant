### Recommendation

- **Premise plausible to be tested:** An optical measurement can detect composition changes while the melt is being processed [S02 p.14; DOCX §3].
- **NIR spectroscopy (diffuse reflection probe).** An AFS extrusion example shows an NIR reflection probe at the die for API in hot polymer [S02 p.14]. Reflection suits scattering media; if the melt is clear, a transmission or transflection geometry should be compared [S01 Table 1 (p.6)].
- **MIR (FTIR) spectroscopy (diamond ATR probe).** The same AFS slide shows API spectra in hot polymer recorded with a diamond ATR probe and FTIR [S02 p.14]. ATR samples only about 1-6 um deep, so a thin layer on the crystal contributes to the signal; contact and fouling matter [S01 Table 1 (p.6); DOCX §5].

**Measurement concept.** Compare NIR diffuse reflection and MIR ATR probes on representative melt samples first [S01 Table 1 (p.6)]. Die or barrel position as in the AFS extrusion example, subject to access and build-up [S02 p.14]. No fiber link stated. Rule check: NIR: Vis-NIR silica partial (0.78-2.4 um); MIR: Vis-NIR silica no coverage; polycrystalline IR (PIR) partial (3-17 um) [S02 p.6; S01 p.10; S01 p.9]. Needs AFS confirmation: Probe temperature/pressure rating, Die or barrel installation, Cleaning or anti-fouling window.

**Spectral range.** NIR spectroscopy: 780–2500 nm nominal [S01 p.10]; MIR (FTIR) ATR: 4000–400 cm-1 nominal [S01 p.9]. Usable range depends on the selected instrument and fiber; no optimal band without sample spectra.

### Risks and uncertainty

- API, polymer or formulation changes can invalidate a calibration [DOCX §5].
- Physical variation such as melt scattering changes can mimic composition changes [DOCX §5].
- Build-up on the probe window may distort spectra. *(assumption)*
- No method or combination is guaranteed to resolve the required changes [DOCX §3].
- Accuracy, update rate, probe ratings and compatibility are unconfirmed [DOCX §3].

### First feasibility experiment

1. Prepare melts across the client-agreed API range and relevant temperature, throughput and formulation variation.
2. Record NIR and ATR spectra with time-aligned offline assay results.
3. Build single-method models first; validate on held-out runs or batches.
4. Compare prediction error, bias and ability to follow changes against client requirements.

**Decision basis.** Agree numerical acceptance criteria with the client before testing; choose the simplest configuration meeting them on independent runs.
