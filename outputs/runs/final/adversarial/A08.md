# A08 · First technical assessment

**Decision status.** Provisional feasibility assessment. Target, matrix and range are defined; method fit and fiber link need testing.

**Requirements stated.** Water in a clear ethanol-water stream, 0.5–5 % w/w, solvent recovery; DN50 stainless steel line, 25 °C; Analyzer in a control room about 20 m away; value every minute; Reference: lab Karl Fischer titration.

**Key unknowns.** Other components and levels; Required accuracy; Karl Fischer uncertainty and timing; Installation, probe contact, fouling.

3. Do bubbles, turbidity, temperature or composition vary between operating states?
4. What accuracy is needed, where can a probe contact the liquid, and is fouling expected?
5. Which cable route exists, and which decision will the value drive?

### Recommendation

- **Premise unverifiable:** The analyzer can sit in a control room about 20 m from the measuring point [S02 p.7].
- **Premise plausible to be tested:** Lab Karl Fischer titration is an adequate reference for the online value.
- **Premise plausible to be tested:** An updated water value every minute is achievable [S01 p.10].
- **NIR spectroscopy (transmission or transflection probe).** AFS materials name NIR transmission for clear liquids; the dairy example is a reason to test [S01 p.18]. The optical pathlength must be precisely controlled [S01 p.18].
- **MIR (FTIR) spectroscopy (ATR probe).** AFS shows MIR-ATR for water in petroleum, a comparison candidate for water in liquids [S02 p.13]. Needs mid-IR fibers, not silica; the 20 m link needs confirmation [S02 p.6].

**Measurement concept.** NIR transmission or transflection probe; MIR-ATR probe as comparison [S01 Table 1 (p.6)]. In the DN50 line or a bypass, analyzer in the control room; point to be agreed. No fiber link stated. Rule check: NIR: Vis-NIR silica partial (0.78-2.4 um); MIR: Vis-NIR silica no coverage; polycrystalline IR (PIR) partial (3-17 um) [S02 p.6; S01 p.10; S01 p.9]. Needs AFS confirmation: Probe type and pathlength, Installation in DN50 line, Fiber link over 20 m.

**Spectral range.** NIR spectroscopy: 780–2500 nm nominal [S01 p.10]; MIR (FTIR) spectroscopy: 4000–400 cm-1 nominal [S01 p.9]. Usable range depends on the selected instrument and fiber; no optimal band without sample spectra.

### Risks and uncertainty

- Other components or matrix changes can invalidate the calibration [DOCX §5].
- Temperature shifts or bubbles can mimic water changes. *(general knowledge)*
- Neither method nor any combination is guaranteed to help. *(assumption)*
- Accuracy, update rate, interface ratings and compatibility are unconfirmed. *(assumption)*
- Fiber family and 20 m link are undefined, especially for mid-IR [S02 p.6].

### First feasibility experiment

1. Collect samples across 0.5–5 % w/w, including temperature, impurity and bubble variation.
2. Record spectra from each candidate with independent Karl Fischer references and timing information.
3. Build single-method models first, combined only if justified; validate on independent runs.
4. Compare prediction error, bias and ability to follow changes against client requirements.

**Decision basis.** Agree numerical acceptance criteria with the client before testing; choose the simplest configuration meeting them on independent material.
