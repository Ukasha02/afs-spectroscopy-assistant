### Recommendation

- **Premise unverifiable:** The analyzer can sit about 20 m away in a control room, linked to the measuring point [S02 p.7; S02 p.6].
- **Premise plausible to be tested:** An updated water value every minute is achievable [S01 p.10].
- **NIR spectroscopy (transmission or transflection probe).** The dairy example supports NIR transmission for transparent liquids such as whey or permeate; a reason to test for this clear stream [S01 p.18]. The optical path length must be set on real samples to avoid signal loss or unrepresentative sampling [S01 p.18].
- **MIR (FTIR) spectroscopy (ATR probe).** An AFS slide shows ATR spectra of oil with 0.1-2.0 % water; a reason to test [S02 p.13]. Silica fibers end at about 2400 nm and no mid-IR fiber link length is given, so a 20 m link is unconfirmed [S02 p.6].

**Measurement concept.** NIR transmission or transflection probe for liquids (1-10 mm path); exact path set on real samples [S01 Table 1 (p.6); S01 p.18]. In the DN50 line at 25 °C; analyzer in the control room about 20 m away. No link stated. Fiber chart: Vis-NIR silica 350-2400 nm; polycrystalline IR 3000 nm-17 um [S02 p.6]. No link stated; MIR needs an IR fiber such as chalcogenide or polycrystalline, not silica [S02 p.6]. Needs AFS confirmation: Probe type and path length, Installation and hazardous-area rating, Fiber link over 20 m.

**Spectral range.** NIR spectroscopy: 780-2500 nm nominal [S01 p.10]; MIR (FTIR) spectroscopy: 4000-400 cm-1 nominal [S01 p.9]. Usable range depends on the selected instrument and fiber; no optimal band without sample spectra.

### Risks and uncertainty

- Overlapping NIR bands need chemometrics; other stream components may affect the model [S01 p.10].
- Temperature or bubbles can distort the water signal. *(general knowledge)*
- No method or combination is guaranteed to meet the requirements. *(assumption)*
- Accuracy, update rate, interface ratings and compatibility are unconfirmed. *(assumption)*
- Fiber ranges are given, but no mid-IR fiber link lengths [S02 p.6].

### First feasibility experiment

1. Collect process samples across 0.5-5 % w/w, covering temperature, bubbles and composition variation.
2. Record NIR and MIR-ATR spectra with independent Karl Fischer reference results and timing information.
3. Build single-method models first; validate on held-out runs or independent batches.
4. Compare prediction error, bias and the ability to follow changes against the client's requirements.

**Decision basis.** Agree numerical acceptance criteria with the client before testing; choose the simplest configuration meeting them on independent material.
