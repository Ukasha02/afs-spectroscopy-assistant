### Risks and uncertainty

- Turbid or opaque juice can saturate a transmission signal [S01 p.16].
- No method or combination is guaranteed to track the off taste. *(assumption)*
- Accuracy, update rate, ratings and compatibility are unconfirmed. *(assumption)*
- The materials contain no juice or taste application. *(assumption)*
- Juice type or blend changes can invalidate a calibration. *(general knowledge)*

### First feasibility experiment

1. Screen retained good and off-taste batches with several methods to find a distinguishing marker and spectral range.
2. Record spectra with independent sensory or lab reference results and sampling times.
3. Build single-method models first, combined only if justified; validate on held-out batches.
4. Compare prediction error, bias and ability to follow changes against client requirements.

**Decision basis.** Agree numerical acceptance and false-reject criteria before testing; choose the simplest configuration meeting them on independent batches.
