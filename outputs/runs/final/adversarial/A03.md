### Recommendation

- **Premise contradicted:** The paper's combined MIR-ATR and fluorescence probe can be mounted over the conveyor to grade carcasses by fat [S04 p.4; S01 Table 1 (p.6)].
- **Premise contradicted:** The method is already validated and can be presented to customers as validated [S04 p.3; DOCX §3].
- **NIR spectroscopy (diffuse reflection, surface at a distance).** Dairy evidence supports NIR reflection for opaque products and NIR allows non-contact measurement; transfer to carcasses is untested [S01 p.17; S01 p.10]. In dairy, reflection is sensitive to surface characteristics; carcass surfaces need testing [S01 p.17].
- **MIR spectroscopy (ATR probe (contact)).** A small lab study, three samples per chicken tissue type, found IR better for fat discrimination [S04 p.4; S04 p.3]. ATR needs tight contact and samples micrometres deep; the fiber must transmit the mid-IR range [S04 p.4; S01 Table 1 (p.6); S02 p.6].

**Measurement concept.** Proposal: NIR diffuse reflection at a distance; MIR ATR probe as lab contact comparison [S01 Table 1 (p.6)]. Lab samples first; conveyor position only after results. No fiber link stated; NIR suits silica, MIR needs a mid-IR fiber such as PIR [S02 p.6]. No fiber link stated. Rule check: NIR: Vis-NIR silica partial (0.78-2.4 um); MIR: Vis-NIR silica no coverage; polycrystalline IR (PIR) partial (3-17 um) [S02 p.6; S01 p.10; S01 p.9]. Needs AFS confirmation: Probe type and standoff, Hygienic installation and cleaning, Optical configuration.

**Spectral range.** NIR spectroscopy: 780–2500 nm nominal [S01 p.10] (Nominal; usable bands from sample spectra); MIR spectroscopy (ATR): 4000–400 cm-1 nominal [S01 p.9] (Usable range set by fiber and crystal). Usable range depends on the selected instrument and fiber; no optimal band without sample spectra.

### Risks and uncertainty

- A change of carcass type or matrix can invalidate a calibration [DOCX §5].
- Surface characteristics alter reflection signals, as shown for dairy [S01 p.17].
- Neither method nor a combination is guaranteed to grade fat. *(assumption)*
- Accuracy, update rate, hygiene ratings and compatibility are unconfirmed. *(assumption)*
- Contact ATR on carcasses raises fouling and cleaning risk. *(general knowledge)*

### First feasibility experiment

1. Collect carcass samples across the client-agreed fat range and surface, temperature and type variation.
2. Record NIR and MIR-ATR spectra with time-aligned independent reference fat results.
3. Build single-method models first; validate on held-out carcasses or production batches.
4. Compare prediction error, bias and ability to follow changes against the client's grading requirements.

**Decision basis.** Agree numerical acceptance criteria with the client before the test; choose the simplest configuration that meets them on independent carcasses.
