### Recommendation

- **Premise unverifiable:** A sensor mounted on the hopper can detect the cause of the black specks [S01 p.3].
- **Premise unverifiable:** The cause of the black specks is in the polymer pellets.
- **NIR spectroscopy (Diffuse reflection on pellets, lab first).** The AFS example is in-line NIR monitoring of API in hot polymer extrusion; use on pellets is an extrapolation to test: "NIR - Reflex & MIR - ATR - spectroscopy to control polymer extrusion in - line" [S02 p.14]. NIR bands overlap and need chemometrics [S01 p.10].
- **MIR (FTIR) spectroscopy (ATR or surface reflection, lab identification of specks).** The example is in-process diamond ATR FTIR of API in hot polymer, not specks: "Spectra of API in hot polymer obtained with Diamond cone ATR probe coupled to iC10 FTIR" [S02 p.14]. ATR probes sample only 1-6 um, for liquid, paste or soft samples [S01 Table 1 (p.6)].

**Measurement concept.** Lab first: NIR diffuse reflection or integrating sphere on pellets; IR surface or layer measurement on isolated specks [S01 Table 1 (p.6)]. Instrument the hopper only after the speck source is traced. No link stated; derived from S02 p.6 ranges, Vis-NIR silica (350-2400 nm) cannot carry MIR, which needs e.g. PIR (3000 nm-17 um) [S02 p.6]. No fiber link stated. Rule check: NIR: Vis-NIR silica partial (0.78-2.4 um); MIR: Vis-NIR silica no coverage; polycrystalline IR (PIR) partial (3-17 um) [S02 p.6; S01 p.10; S01 p.9]. Needs AFS confirmation: Probe type, Hopper installation, Optical configuration.

**Spectral range.** NIR spectroscopy: 780-2500 nm nominal [S01 p.10]; MIR (FTIR) spectroscopy: 4000-400 cm-1 nominal [S01 p.9]. Usable range depends on the selected instrument and fiber; no optimal band without sample spectra.

### Risks and uncertainty

- No method, single or combined, is guaranteed to detect the specks. *(assumption)*
- Detectability, accuracy, update rate and hopper compatibility are unconfirmed. *(assumption)*
- The polymer example (API in extrusion) is not speck detection [S02 p.14] *(assumption)*.
- A spot-sampling probe may miss sporadic small specks. *(assumption)*

### First feasibility experiment

1. Multi-method lab screening of specks, clean and suspect pellets and defective parts to identify the contaminant and spectral range [S02 p.20].
2. Record spectra per candidate with independent reference analysis and batch timing.
3. Single-method models first, combined only if justified; validate on held-out batches.
4. Compare prediction error, bias and ability to follow changes against client requirements.

**Decision basis.** Agree numerical acceptance criteria with the client before testing; choose the simplest configuration meeting them on independent batches.
