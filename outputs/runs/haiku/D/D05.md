- **Premise: The optical measurement will reveal changes earlier than the current method.** — unverifiable. No source gives lead time for yeast fermentation; it must be measured against the reference method.
- **Premise: A metabolic signal can be followed optically during fermentation.** — plausible to be tested. The metabolic target is not yet defined. S01 p.16 describes Raman as a molecular fingerprint for direct detection of glucose, acetate or lactate [S01 p.16].
- **UV/Vis spectroscopy (Transmission or transflection probe for clear broth).** S01 p.16 describes UV/Vis turbidity as a possible low-cost biomass trend in cell suspensions [S01 p.16]. Signal saturates in opaque broth; UV-ATR is the cited alternative at high optical density [S01 p.16].
