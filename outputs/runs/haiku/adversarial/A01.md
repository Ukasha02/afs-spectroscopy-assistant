### Recommendation

- **Premise: A chalcogenide infrared fiber can serve as the link between the fermenter probe and the UV/Vis spectrometer.** — contradicted. The CIR fiber transmits about 1.1-6.5 µm and does not cover the UV/Vis range [S02 p.6]. UV-visible spectroscopy is used in the 190-360 nm UV region and the visible range [S01 p.5].
- **Premise: The leftover FTIR fiber is suitable for reuse in the new UV/Vis setup.** — contradicted. Its mid-IR range does not match the UV/Vis target; fiber condition and length are unconfirmed [S02 p.6].
- **Premise: Moving the spectrometer to the control room is a workable remote solution.** — plausible to be tested. Robust 100-300 m silica cables exist for remote process control; UV-silica suitability and the client's link length are unknown [S02 p.7].
- **Premise: The leftover fiber length suits the fermenter-to-control-room distance.** — unverifiable. Neither the distance nor the fiber length is stated, so suitability cannot be checked. *(assumption)*
- **UV/Vis absorbance (Transmission or transflection probe; UV-silica link to be defined).** A bioprocess example supports UV/Vis transmission probes: "For highly transparent liquids with low optical density, UV-Transmission or Transflection probes are ideal." [S01 p.16]. Signal saturates in opaque or concentrated broth, so broth optics must be tested [S01 p.16].
- **UV/Vis absorbance (UV/Vis ATR probe).** The bioprocess example describes this option for opaque media: "a UV-ATR (Attenuated Total Reflection) probe is used" [S01 p.16]. Rated low in sensitivity and selectivity; comparison candidate only [S01 Table 1 (p.6)].

**Measurement concept.** UV/Vis transmission or transflection probe (1-10 mm path) as candidate; UV/Vis ATR as comparison [S01 Table 1 (p.6)]. Probe in the fermenter; spectrometer in control room. CIR fiber cannot carry UV/Vis; UV-silica link (190-1200 nm) proposed, length unknown [S02 p.6]. Rule check of the stated fiber: UVVIS: Chalcogenide As-S IR fiber (CIR) none [S02 p.6]. Needs AFS confirmation: Probe type and sterilisation rating, Link length and routing, Optical configuration for broth.

**Spectral range.** UV/Vis spectroscopy: UV 190-360 nm and visible; depends on target and setup nominal [S01 p.5]; UV-Vis silica fiber (proposed link): 190-1200 nm nominal [S02 p.6] (Client's CIR covers 1.1-6.5 µm instead). Usable range depends on the selected instrument and fiber; no optimal band without sample spectra.

### Risks and uncertainty

- CIR fiber cannot transmit UV/Vis; reuse is not possible [S02 p.6].
- A change in by-product or broth composition can invalidate the calibration [DOCX §5].
- Turbidity and biomass can mimic absorbance changes [S01 p.16].
- Neither UV/Vis nor a combination is guaranteed to suit this broth. *(general knowledge)*
- Accuracy, response time, ratings and compatibility are unconfirmed. *(assumption)*

### First feasibility experiment

1. Collect broth across the agreed concentration range and fermentation stages, including colour and biomass variation.
2. Record UV/Vis transmission and ATR spectra with independent lab references and sampling times.
3. Build single-method models first; validate on held-out batches before any combination.
4. Compare prediction error, bias and ability to follow changes against client requirements.

**Decision basis.** Agree accuracy, response time and link length before testing; choose the simplest configuration meeting them on independent batches.
