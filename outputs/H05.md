### First feasibility experiment

1. Screen retained samples with several methods to find discriminating features, spectral range and level [S02 p.20].
2. Record spectra from each candidate with independent reference results and sampling times.
3. Single-method models first, combined only if justified; validate on held-out batches.
4. Compare prediction error, bias and ability to follow changes against client requirements.

**Decision basis.** Agree numerical acceptance criteria with the client before testing; choose the simplest configuration meeting them on independent batches.
