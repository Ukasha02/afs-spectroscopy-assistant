### Risks and uncertainty

- Temperature, colour or turbidity changes can mimic contamination. *(general knowledge)*
- Sensitive methods may detect a change without identifying the contaminant [S01 Table 1 (p.6)].
- Neither a single method nor a combination is guaranteed to detect it [S01 p.7].
- Detection level, accuracy, update rate and compatibility are all unconfirmed [DOCX §3].

### First feasibility experiment

1. Screen retained in-spec and off-spec samples with several methods to identify target, range and level [S02 p.20].
2. Record spectra per candidate with independent, time-aligned reference results.
3. Single-method models first; combine only if justified; validate on held-out batches.
4. Compare prediction error, bias and ability to follow changes against the client's requirements.

**Decision basis.** Agree numerical acceptance criteria with the client before testing; choose the simplest configuration that meets them on independent batches.
