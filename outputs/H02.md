### Recommendation

- **Premise plausible to be tested:** Laboratory protein and fat results can serve as the reference for composition monitoring [S01 p.17].
- **NIR spectroscopy (whey stream) (transmission or transflection probe).** The dairy example names NIR transmission for clear streams such as whey [S01 p.18]. The optical pathlength must be set on real samples [S01 p.18].
- **NIR spectroscopy (milk concentrate) (diffuse reflection probe).** The dairy example uses NIR reflection for opaque concentrates [S01 p.17]. The signal depends on surface characteristics and fat globule size [S01 p.17].
- **NIR spectroscopy (both streams) (transflection probe).** One transflection probe for both streams is a comparison candidate [S01 p.18]. Transmission fails for thick or fat-rich product; testing on the concentrate is needed [S01 p.18].

**Measurement concept.** NIR transmission or transflection on whey, diffuse reflection on the concentrate; transflection also tested on both [S01 Table 1 (p.6); S01 p.18]. Lab screening first, then flush-mounted probes at client-confirmed points per stream [S01 p.17]. No fiber link stated; fiber type and length need confirmation. *(assumption)* No fiber link stated. Rule check: NIR: Vis-NIR silica partial (0.78-2.4 um) [S02 p.6; S01 p.10]. Needs AFS confirmation: Probe type per stream, Installation and cleaning compatibility, Optical pathlength configuration.

**Spectral range.** NIR spectroscopy: 780–2500 nm nominal [S01 p.10]. Usable range depends on the selected instrument and fiber; no optimal band without sample spectra.

### Risks and uncertainty

- Product or formulation changes can invalidate a calibration. *(general knowledge)*
- Fat globule size and scattering affect reflection signals, not only composition [S01 p.17].
- No geometry, nor a shared probe, is guaranteed to work. *(assumption)*
- Accuracy, update rate, interface ratings and compatibility are unconfirmed. *(assumption)*
- Window fouling during production and cleaning is unassessed. *(assumption)*

### First feasibility experiment

1. Collect whey and concentrate across the agreed range, including temperature, homogenisation and bubble variation.
2. Record spectra per interface with time-aligned laboratory protein and fat results.
3. Build single-method models per stream and geometry first; validate on held-out runs or batches.
4. Compare prediction error, bias and ability to follow changes against client requirements.

**Decision basis.** Agree numerical acceptance criteria before testing; choose the simplest interface per stream meeting them on independent material.
