## Recommendation

### 1. NIR — Diffuse reflection probe (contact or close proximity to powder stream) (`primary`, cost average, confidence high)
- *Spectral range (nominal):* 780-2500 nm (nominal method range)
- NIR diffuse reflection is the most common online analysis method performed using fiber optic probes, capturing overtone and combination vibrations of C-H, O-H, and N-H bonds. `[source: S01 p.10; S01 Table 1 (p.6)]`
- It provides a robust, cost-effective interface with standard quartz optics and silica fibers, fitting industrial process constraints. `[source: S01 p.5]`
- *Condition:* Requires that the API exhibits distinct spectral absorption bands separate from excipients and has an adequate concentration level (typically above ~0.1%). `[source: S01 p.16]`
- *Range note:* 780-2500 nm is the nominal method range; the usable operational range depends on the spectrometer detector and silica fiber transmission window. `[source: DOCX §5]`
- *Confidence:* Directly supported by literature for blend potency monitoring in tablet press feed frames.

### 2. Raman — Raman immersion/touch fiber probe (e.g. 785 nm laser excitation) (`alternative`, cost high, confidence medium)
- *Spectral range (nominal):* Raman shift in cm-1; signal recorded in 400-1100 nm (laser excitation stated separately)
- Raman scattering provides high molecular selectivity and sharp fingerprint bands corresponding to non-polar bonds and aromatic backbones of organic API molecules. `[source: S01 p.11; S01 Table 1 (p.6)]`
- Linear dependence on analyte concentration across wide intensity ranges makes Raman highly suitable for quantitative blend potency tracking. `[source: S01 p.12]`
- *Why not simpler:* Raman involves higher instrument cost than NIR and is vulnerable to laser-induced sample fluorescence background from excipients or impurities. `[source: S01 Table 1 (p.6); S01 p.11]`
- *Condition:* Requires checking that laser heating does not degrade the powder blend and that sample autofluorescence does not swamp the Raman signal. `[general knowledge]`
- *Confidence:* High selectivity, but feasibility depends heavily on fluorescence background and laser power safety in a moving powder bed.

### 3. NIR + Raman — Multispectral combi fiber probe (e.g. NIRaman probe mounted in feed frame) (`add_on_if_needed`, cost high, confidence medium)
- Combining NIR reflection and Raman scattering captures complementary chemical information (polar hydrogen bonding vs molecular backbone) from the same powder volume. `[source: S01 p.15]`
- Low-level data fusion with PLS regression has demonstrated higher accuracy and robustness than single-method models for feed frame blend potency. `[source: S01 p.15]`
- *Combine only if:* Combine methods only if single-method NIR or Raman models fail to achieve required prediction accuracy or fail across varying excipient lots. `[source: DOCX §2 D01]`
- *Why not simpler:* Deploying two spectrometers and a specialized combi probe significantly increases capital cost, mechanical complexity, and data alignment challenges. `[source: S01 p.8; S01 p.9]`
- *Confidence:* Documented in AFS literature, but combination gain is a hypothesis that must be proven against single-method baselines.

## Measurement concept

- **Probe:** Diffuse reflection fiber probe (or compact NIRaman combi probe if combined) · **Geometry:** Flush-mounted optical window or probe tip contacting the flowing powder stream in the feed frame · **Location:** Feed frame channel of the rotary tablet press · **Architecture:** undecided
- **Fiber link:** Low-OH Vis-NIR silica fiber cables (e.g. metal-coated or industrial armored) (compatible: True)
- Silica fibers transmit efficiently across the Vis-NIR range (350-2400 nm), covering the wavelengths needed for NIR spectroscopy and Raman detection. `[source: S02 p.6; S02 p.7]`
- Locating the probe in the feed frame allows real-time measurement immediately prior to die filling, while fiber links isolate sensitive spectrometers from press vibration and dust. `[source: S01 p.15]`

## Risks and uncertainty

- **[technical]** Physical variability in the powder blend (particle size distribution, bulk density, paddle-induced compaction) can introduce severe baseline shifts in NIR diffuse reflection. → *Mitigation:* Apply chemometric spectral preprocessing such as standard normal variate (SNV) or multiplicative scatter correction (MSC) and evaluate spectral derivatives. `[source: S01 p.17; DOCX §5]`
- **[technical]** Powder caking or electrostatically adhering to the optical probe window in the feed frame can degrade signal and obscure flowing material changes. → *Mitigation:* Design flush-mounted, polished sapphire window interfaces with optimized probe immersion geometry or non-contact optical sight glasses. `[general knowledge]`
- **[technical]** Fluorescence background from organic excipients or trace impurities can saturate the detector during Raman measurements. → *Mitigation:* Screen blend samples in the lab to evaluate fluorescence background under laser excitation before committing to Raman hardware. `[source: S01 p.11]`
- **[assumption]** The API concentration may be below the detection limit of NIR spectroscopy (<0.1%) or obscured by overlapping excipient bands. → *Mitigation:* Quantify signal-to-noise and detection limits during initial lab screening across representative concentration gradients. `[source: S01 p.16]`
- **[evidence_scope]** Demonstrated success of blend potency monitoring in published literature does not guarantee model transferability to the client's proprietary formulation. → *Mitigation:* Calibrations are strictly matrix-specific; develop and validate empirical chemometric models on the client's actual production materials. `[source: DOCX §3; DOCX §5]`

## Feasibility plan

**Objective:** Screen and compare fiber-optic NIR and Raman spectroscopy for real-time API concentration tracking in tablet blend formulations, establishing single-method limits before considering data fusion.

- Methods screened in the lab: NIR spectroscopy (diffuse reflection), Raman spectroscopy (785 nm excitation)
- Methods compared: NIR spectroscopy, Raman spectroscopy

**1. Lab screening**
- *samples:* Acquire representative blend samples spanning the intended API concentration range, including intentional target, low, and high potency variations, alongside individual pure API and excipient lots. `[source: DOCX §2 D01]`
- *establish:* Establish spectral feature visibility, optimum wavelength ranges, required spectral resolution, sensitivity level (% vs ppm), and check for Raman fluorescence interference. `[source: S01 p.11; S02 p.20]`

**2. Method comparison against the reference**
- *reference method:* Analyze all screening samples with the client's existing laboratory reference assay (e.g. HPLC) to obtain time-aligned benchmark concentration values. `[source: DOCX §2 D01]`
- *compare on:* Construct single-method chemometric models (PLS regression) for NIR and Raman independently; evaluate prediction error (RMSEP), bias, and robustness on independent validation blends. `[source: DOCX §2 D01]`
- *decision basis:* Select the simplest sufficient single method that meets the client's pre-agreed precision and accuracy criteria; evaluate combined NIR + Raman data fusion only if both single methods fall short. `[source: DOCX §2 D01]`

**3. In-line / in-situ trial**
- *location:* Install the selected fiber-optic probe into the feed frame of the tablet press. `[source: S01 p.15]`
- *interface:* Integrate a flush probe interface designed to minimize powder disruption and window blinding under active paddle agitation. `[source: S01 p.15]`
- *verify:* Verify dynamic tracking of API concentration changes during active press operation against grab samples tested with the reference laboratory assay. `[source: S01 p.15]`

**4. Scale-up**
- *robustness:* Assess long-term optical baseline drift, mechanical vibration tolerance, and ambient light isolation under commercial tablet manufacturing conditions. `[source: S01 p.15]`
- *cleaning sterilization:* Establish feed frame probe removal, clean-in-place/wipe-down procedures, and compatibility with pharmaceutical cleaning solvents. `[general knowledge]`
- *industry standards:* Align hardware housing, materials of contact, and chemometric data software with pharmaceutical GMP, PAT, and data integrity guidelines. `[general knowledge]`
- *few wavelength simplification:* Examine whether full-spectrum acquisition can be simplified to a dedicated few-wavelength sensor for cost-effective deployment across multiple tablet presses. `[source: S02 p.4; S02 p.20]`
