### Risks and uncertainty

- A change of API, excipients or formulation can invalidate the calibration [DOCX §5].
- Particle size, density or flow can change the signal [DOCX §5].
- A combination's gain is a hypothesis to test, not guaranteed [DOCX §5].
- Accuracy, response time, probe ratings and equipment compatibility are unconfirmed. *(assumption)*
- Fiber link length and feed-frame access are unknown. *(assumption)*

### First feasibility experiment

1. Prepare blends across the client-agreed API range and feed-frame variation (flow, density, particle size).
2. Record NIR and Raman spectra with time-aligned reference assays from the same blend passage.
3. Build single-method models first; combine only if justified; validate on held-out batches.
4. Compare prediction error, bias and ability to follow changes against the client's requirements.

**Decision basis.** Agree accuracy, bias and response-time criteria with the client before testing; choose the simplest configuration meeting them on independent batches.
