### Recommendation

- **Premise: A fiber-optic probe in the distribution pipe can measure trace lead at ppb level continuously.** — unverifiable. S01 p.16 says NIR may lack sensitivity for trace compounds below 0.1 percent; no lead or ppb data are given [S01 p.16]. No lead-in-water application appears in the AFS materials reviewed. *(assumption)*
- **Premise: An immersed fiber probe can run continuously in a distribution pipe.** — plausible to be tested. Probe-based analysis is used for in-line, in-process liquid measurement [S01 p.10]. Pressure, fouling and sealing in the pipe are unconfirmed. *(assumption)*
- **Not recommended: Fluorimetry (Fluorimetric probe).** Fluorimetry relies on fluorescence, and most chemicals do not show it; no lead fluorophore is documented [S01 p.5]. Fluorimetry is primarily used in medicine, biology and biotechnology [S01 p.5].
- **Not recommended: NIR spectroscopy (Transmission or transflection probe).** Transmission probes suit clear liquids, but NIR may lack sensitivity for trace compounds (S01 p.16); table ratings are qualitative [S01 p.16; S01 Table 1 (p.6)]. NIR may lack the sensitivity to detect trace compounds below 0.1 percent [S01 p.16].

**Measurement concept.** Transmission or transflection probe suits clear liquids; sources do not document ppb lead detection with it [S01 Table 1 (p.6)]. Distribution pipe, possibly pressurized; contact possible. Analyzer distance and fiber link unknown; no fiber length assumed. *(assumption)* Needs AFS confirmation: Lead detectability at ppb, Pipe pressure and fouling suitability, Analyzer distance and fiber link.

**Spectral range.** NIR spectroscopy: 780-2500 nm nominal [S01 p.10]; Raman spectroscopy: Raman shift in cm-1; signal recorded in 400-1100 nm nominal [S01 p.11] (Excitation wavelength not stated; state separately if tested). Usable range depends on the selected instrument and fiber; no optimal band without sample spectra.

### Risks and uncertainty

- A change of matrix or lead form can invalidate any calibration [DOCX §5].
- Temperature, scattering and flow can mimic composition changes [DOCX §5].
- Neither a method nor a combination is guaranteed to help here. *(assumption)*
- Accuracy, update rate, interface ratings and compatibility are unconfirmed. *(assumption)*
- A pipe-mounted probe may foul; sealing and cleaning are unconfirmed. *(assumption)*

### First feasibility experiment

1. Prepare drinking-water samples spiked across the client-agreed lead range, with blanks and matrix variation.
2. Record spectra from each candidate with independent lab lead results and timing information.
3. Build single-method models first; validate on held-out batches before any combination is considered.
4. Compare prediction error, bias and ability to follow changes against the client's requirements.

**Decision basis.** Agree lead detection limit, accuracy and response time with the client first; choose the simplest configuration meeting them on independent samples.
